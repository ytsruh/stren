## Intro

Getting stronger takes time. Seeing that you're getting stronger shouldn't.
Stren makes it effortless to log every set, then turns your training into clear,
encouraging progress you can actually see. No clutter, no noise — just you,
your lifts, and the numbers moving in the right direction.

## Features

### Log every set in seconds
Reps, weight, rest time, notes. Add a whole set of sets in one go, right between
exercises. Logging shouldn't get in the way of your workout — with Stren, it doesn't.

### Watch your progress happen
Every set you log builds your story. Personal bests are spotted automatically,
progression charts show your heaviest lifts climbing over time, and each exercise
gets its own history page so you always know where you stand.

### Track your weight journey
Log weigh-ins with optional progress photos, set a target weight, and compare
before and after whenever you like. Optional email reminders keep you consistent —
daily, weekly, or fortnightly, on your schedule.

### Set goals. Smash them.
Whether it's hitting a new bench press PR or sticking to a routine, set goals with
target dates and tick them off as you go. Built-in countdown and EMOM timers keep
your rest periods honest, too.
